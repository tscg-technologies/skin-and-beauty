Yes, absolutely. A preliminary consultation is essential. It enables the professional to assess your skin barrier, evaluate allergies, review medical history, and verify that the intended procedure is completely safe and appropriate for your individual physiology.

Not all treatments suit every skin type. For example, deeper Indian skin types (Fitzpatrick IV–VI) require gentle, non-aggressive chemical peel formulas (such as mandelic or lactic acid) to prevent post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation. Suitability is always determined individually.

You do not need to choose in advance. We assess whether your skin requires barrier hydration, enzymatic decongestion, calming soothing agents, or antioxidant brightening during your consultation, and customize the facial session accordingly.

Chemical peels are not suitable for individuals with active open sores, sunburn, active eczema or dermatitis flares, or those currently taking certain oral retinoids. Mild peels are generally well tolerated when preceded by an individual clinical assessment.

Avoid strong active exfoliants (like prescription retinoids, high-percentage glycolic acid, or physical facial scrubs) for 3 to 5 days prior to your session. Avoid direct tanning or prolonged sun exposure, and arrive with clean skin if possible.

Downtime varies depending on the modality. Relaxing clinical facials and hydration therapies involve zero downtime. Superficial chemical peels and microneedling may present slight pinkness or mild tightness for 24 to 48 hours, easily managed with soothing barrier creams.

Aesthetic skin care works progressively with your biological cellular turnover. While hydration facials produce immediate radiance, addressing persistent acne marks, deep texture, or pigmentation typically involves a structured series of 3 to 6 sessions spaced several weeks apart.
